- The smart button **Waiting List** on the event shows the list in queue
  order. Reorder entries with drag and drop.
- **Move up** on an entry offers a seat to (or registers) this person right
  away, if seats are free. **Move up waiting list** on the event gives all
  free seats to the list (this also happens automatically).
- On an offered entry, staff can **Confirm seat** or **Decline seat** on
  behalf of the person.
- *Events > Reporting > Waiting Lists* shows the entries of all events.
