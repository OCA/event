Waiting list for sold-out events (courses, classes, workshops).

When an event or a ticket is fully booked, people can join a waiting
list. As soon as a seat becomes free (a registration is cancelled,
archived or deleted, or the capacity is increased), the waiting list
moves up automatically, in one of two modes chosen per event:

-  **Offer the seat** (default): the first person receives an email with
   a link and has a deadline (12 hours by default) to accept. The seat
   is reserved for them meanwhile and cannot be booked by anyone else.
   If the deadline passes or the person declines, the next person gets
   the offer.
-  **Register automatically**: the first person is registered right away
   and receives the normal registration confirmation.

Other features:

-  One waiting list per event; an entry may target a specific ticket.
-  People asking for several seats keep their position; a freed seat
   goes to the first person whose request fits.
-  No offers after the event has started; the deadline never goes beyond
   the start of the event.
-  Editable email templates: entry confirmed (with the position), seat
   offered, seat confirmed, offer expired. Every email contains a link
   to leave the list.
-  Token links for attendees (no record ids in URLs). Links in emails
   only open a page; actions require a click on that page, so email
   scanners cannot accept or decline seats by following links.
-  Duplicate entries (same email, same event) are refused.
-  Seat assignment is serialized per event, so two cancellations at the
   same time can never give away more seats than are free or offer one
   seat twice.
-  Entries are deleted automatically a configurable number of days after
   the event (data protection).
-  Every step is logged in the chatter of the event.

The website part (sign-up form, button instead of "Sold Out") is in
``website_event_waitlist``.

Configuration
=============

Open an event, tab **Waiting List**:

-  **Waiting List**: turn it on.
-  **When a Seat Becomes Free**: *Offer the seat (with deadline)* or
   *Register automatically*.
-  **Offer Deadline (hours)**: time to accept an offered seat (default
   12).
-  **Maximum Waiting List Length**: 0 = unlimited.
-  **Delete Entries After (days)**: retention after the end of the
   event.

The emails can be adapted in *Settings > Technical > Email Templates*
(search for "Waiting List").

Two scheduled actions are installed: *expire offers and move up* (every
10 minutes) and *delete entries of past events* (daily).

Usage
=====

-  The smart button **Waiting List** on the event shows the list in
   queue order. Reorder entries with drag and drop.
-  **Move up** on an entry offers a seat to (or registers) this person
   right away, if seats are free. **Move up waiting list** on the event
   gives all free seats to the list (this also happens automatically).
-  On an offered entry, staff can **Confirm seat** or **Decline seat**
   on behalf of the person.
-  *Events > Reporting > Waiting Lists* shows the entries of all events.
